# Demo walkthrough

Use an adult-operated demo with synthetic explanations. Start with a fresh notebook via Grown-ups if you want an empty badge collection.

## Two-minute story

**0:00–0:20 — The learner problem.** “Children can recognize an answer without being able to explain it. Teachback Lab gives them a curious student: Pip.” Show the discovery island and choose The puddle puzzle.

**0:20–0:40 — Discover.** Open the mission. Show the three learning notes and the optional Read to me control. The lesson connects cause and effect with a small sequence diagram.

**0:40–1:10 — Teach and revise.** Submit: “The Sun warms the puddle and the water goes into the air.” Read Pip’s response. If practice mode is on, explicitly say these are authored prompts, not live AI. Revise: “The Sun warms the puddle. Some liquid water changes into water vapor in the air. This is evaporation, like a wet towel drying.” Submit again.

**1:10–1:35 — Transfer.** Choose Try a new challenge. Explain why a towel dries. A wrong answer gives a clue and allows another try. Select “It becomes water vapor in the air.”

**1:35–1:50 — Celebrate and persist.** Show the three effort stars and badge. Open My badges and reload to demonstrate durable progress. Explain that rewards measure participation in the learning loop, not validated mastery.

**1:50–2:00 — How it works.** “React game UI, validated server routes, anonymous D1 notebooks, and a replaceable AI coach adapter grounded in authored lesson facts.” Briefly name the remaining work: model evaluation, curriculum review, and child-privacy launch work.

## Manual checks

- Try an explanation shorter than 20 characters; submission stays disabled.
- Try the wrong transfer answer; no badge is awarded.
- Revise and resubmit; complete the check and confirm three stars.
- Repeat a mission; total rewards do not grow beyond the best saved score.
- Reload; progress remains linked to the browser.
- Open another browser profile; it has a separate notebook.
- Switch between Explore, My badges, and Grown-ups by keyboard.
- Check narrow/mobile layout, reduced motion, and visible focus.
- Test provider timeout/refusal/malformed output before enabling live AI for a demo.
